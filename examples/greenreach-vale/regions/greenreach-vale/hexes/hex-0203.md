Old oak forest opens onto the River Bracken where a shelf of gravel makes the
vale's only reliable ford. [[settlements/bracken-ford|Bracken Ford]] village
sits on the west bank behind a ditch and a mossy palisade; the road from
[[settlements/greenreach|Greenreach]] comes down to the water here and does
not resume on the far side so much as surrender to the trees. Travelers see
eel-traps staked in the shallows, a squat ferry barge for the wet months, and
the Lantern Vigil's chapel light burning on the bank after dark.
