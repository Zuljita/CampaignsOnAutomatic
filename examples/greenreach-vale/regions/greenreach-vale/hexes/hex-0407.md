<!-- oa:generated begin section="description" -->
The River Bracken drops a man's height over a lip of black stone and goes
under the peat — the Mirefall, the only waterfall in the vale that falls
*into* the ground. The plunge pool never silts up and never gives back what
sinks. On the north bank, half-drowned steps lead down to the sinkmouth of
[[sites/mirefall-warren/site|Mirefall Warren]]. Since spring the steps are
swept clean, and toll-stakes topped with left boots stand along the old
causeway approach.
<!-- oa:generated end -->

<!-- oa:generated begin section="encounters" -->
By day, warband toll-parties from the Warren watch the causeway (use the
roster in [[sites/mirefall-warren/key|the site key]]). At dusk the sinkmouth
exhales bats — `doa_bat`, in nuisance clouds — which is how the warband times
its watches. If delvers camp within earshot of the falls, one sentry per
night dreams of the thing in the plunge pool; treat any attempt to dive it
as an audience with `enraged_eggplant_aboleth`, which is *not* scaled for a
starting party and should be foreshadowed, not sprung.
