<!-- oa:generated begin section="description" -->
The oaks here grow wrong — spiral-grained, thorned along the trunk, crowded
close enough to shut out noon. Charcoal-burners abandoned this hex a
generation ago and their collapsed clamps dot the understory like burial
mounds. Every game trail in the hex curves, gently and without exception,
toward the same dark stand at its heart.
<!-- oa:generated end -->

<!-- oa:generated begin section="encounters" -->
Nothing here uses a roster; the wood itself is the encounter. Travelers who
keep to the edges lose an hour and a temper. Travelers who cut toward the
center find their own back-trail thorned over and one member of the party
briefly *elsewhere* — returned unharmed, smelling of sap, unwilling to talk
about it. Treat [[factions/thornwake|the Thornwake]] as weather until the GM
