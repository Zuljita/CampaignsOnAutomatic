<!-- oa:generated begin section="description" -->
The old mire road runs through this hex on a causeway of rammed peat and
reused gravestones, now flooded to ankle depth for most of its length. Way-
posts every hundred paces carry iron lantern hooks — all empty. Where the
causeway dips fully under, locals have staked willow withies to mark the
solid line; some of the withies have been *moved*.
<!-- oa:generated end -->

<!-- oa:generated begin section="encounters" -->
The hazard here is the marsh itself: re-staked withies lead a careless file
of travelers off the causeway into bottomless peat (one bad step, then a
rescue scene, not a drowning — unless armored). Roll twice per crossing
rather than once. Any encounter with the warband here starts at bow-shot
across open water, which is exactly how they like it.
