# Bracken Ford

GM note: the village is the party's base. Keep it warm, keep it small, and
let the players learn everyone's name — it makes the toll-stakes personal.

<!-- oa:generated begin section="overview" -->
Three hundred souls on the west bank of the only good crossing in the vale.
Bracken Ford lives on eels, peat, and ferry-pence, and it is governed by
whoever [[npcs/hedda-carse|Reeve Hedda Carse]] cannot talk out of the room.
The palisade is older than anyone's grandmother and mended in eleven styles.
Since the mire road closed, the village is quietly overstocked with boats
and understocked with nerve: everyone knows the warband's toll-stakes are
two hexes upriver, and everyone is waiting for someone else's answer.
<!-- oa:generated end -->

<!-- oa:generated begin section="locations" -->
- **The Eel and Lantern** — the only inn; the taproom doubles as the moot
  hall. [[npcs/skarn-halfweight|Skarn Halfweight]] keeps the back-corner
  table and, less publicly, the back-corner ledger.
- **Chapel of the Vigil** — bankside chapel of the
  [[factions/lantern-vigil|Lantern Vigil]]; its lamp is the last lit link in
  the wayshrine line. [[npcs/serrin-talvane|Abbess Serrin Talvane]] keeps it
  burning and keeps the signal-log herself.
- **The Reeve's Yard** — tithe barn, lockup, and the ford-toll counting
  table, all under one turf roof.
- **Stake Row** — the eel-drying racks along the bank; the closest thing the
  village has to a market, busiest an hour after the traps come up.
