# Greenreach Vale

A wet green valley on the wrong side of the tin hills, three days off any road
that matters. The vale grows barley, cuts peat, and minds its own business —
until the spring floods washed something loose under the Mirefall and the
warband that crawled out started tolling the causeway.

This is a starter sandbox for GURPS Dungeon Fantasy: one region map, two
settlements, one dungeon, and a knot of small powers pulling against each
other. Start the delvers in [[settlements/bracken-ford|Bracken Ford]], let
[[npcs/hedda-carse|the reeve]] point them at the mire, and let
[[npcs/skarn-halfweight|Skarn]]'s ledger complicate everything afterward.

**Tone:** mud, lantern-light, small stakes that matter. Nobody here is saving
the world; everybody is trying to keep the ford open through winter.

**Where to begin reading:** [[regions/greenreach-vale/region|the region
gazetteer]], then [[sites/mirefall-warren/site|Mirefall Warren]].
