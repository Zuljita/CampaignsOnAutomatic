# Greenreach Vale

A wet green valley on the wrong side of the tin hills, three days off any road
that matters. The vale grows barley, cuts peat, and minds its own business —
until the spring floods washed something loose under the Mirefall and the
warband that crawled out started tolling the causeway.

This is a starter sandbox for GURPS Dungeon Fantasy: one region map, two
settlements, one dungeon, and a knot of small powers pulling against each
other. Start the delvers in [[settlements/bracken-ford|Bracken Ford]], let
[[npcs/hedda-carse|the reeve]] point them at the mire, and let
[[npcs/skarn-halfweight|Skarn]]'s ledger complicate everything afterward.

**Tone:** mud, lantern-light, small stakes that matter. Nobody here is saving
the world; everybody is trying to keep the ford open through winter.

**Where to begin reading:** [[regions/greenreach-vale/region|the region
gazetteer]], then [[sites/mirefall-warren/site|Mirefall Warren]].

### 2. Where does the party buy ordinary adventuring gear?

Stake Row in [[settlements/bracken-ford|Bracken Ford]], which is eel racks and
whatever the ferry brought — rope, oil, sacking, a bad axe. Anything above a
woodsman's kit is a special order through
[[npcs/skarn-halfweight|Skarn Halfweight]], paid up front, arriving whenever
the plains road dries. Real stock waits at [[settlements/greenreach|Greenreach]],
three days east.

### 7. Where can the party get magical healing?

The Chapel of the Vigil on the Bracken Ford bank.
[[npcs/serrin-talvane|Abbess Serrin Talvane]] will lay hands on anyone who
reaches her, and asks for lamp oil rather than coin — but the
[[factions/lantern-vigil|Vigil]] is four professed members thin, and she is
also the only one who keeps the signal-log.

### 13. Which way to the nearest tavern?

The Eel and Lantern, the only inn in the vale, whose taproom is also the moot
hall. Skarn keeps the back-corner table.

### 14. What is terrorizing the countryside badly enough that killing it makes a name?

The warband that came up out of the [[sites/mirefall-warren|Mirefall Warren]]
after the spring floods and started tolling the causeway. Break the toll and
the vale will know the party's names by harvest.

### 18. What is there to eat around here?

Eel, six ways, most of them smoked over peat. Barley bread, barley beer, and
barley porridge. Anything that grew above the waterline is a delicacy and
priced accordingly.

### 21. Who do we pay when the ford floods?

[[npcs/hedda-carse|The reeve]], in barley, and she remembers exactly who was
short.
