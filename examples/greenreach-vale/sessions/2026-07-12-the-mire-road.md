# Session 1 — The Mire Road (2026-07-12)

**Present:** full table. **In-world:** three days, late spring.

The party took [[npcs/hedda-carse|Reeve Carse]]'s stake-bounty at the Eel
and Lantern, then immediately complicated it by letting
[[npcs/skarn-halfweight|Skarn Halfweight]] buy the second round. Skarn
offered "a friend's advice" about which causeway withies to trust — accurate,
which nobody at the table found reassuring.

Upriver by boat, then the drowned causeway. One near-drowning at the moved
withies (Barrow's player rolled the rescue of the campaign so far). At dusk
they watched the bat-flight from the sinkmouth and correctly clocked it as
the warren's watch-bell.

First contact with [[factions/mirefall-warband|the warband]] went to parley:
a toll-party of six, very bored, entirely willing to describe the toll
schedule. The party paid in coin and information — they now know the
warband will not go below its own cellar, and the warband knows the party
asked. Session ended camped in sight of the falls, with the light in
[[factions/lantern-vigil|the Vigil]]'s chapel behind them and none ahead.

**Next time:** the gate of [[sites/mirefall-warren/site|Mirefall Warren]],
and whether to ring the bell.

**GM bookkeeping:** revealed the truce-shaped hole in the Vigil's
signal-log to no one yet; [[npcs/serrin-talvane|the abbess]] deflected one
direct question. Elowen Pell not yet mentioned at the table.
