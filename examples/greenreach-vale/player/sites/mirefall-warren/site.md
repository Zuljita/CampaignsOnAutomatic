Mirefall Warren is a half-flooded warren of worked stone and dug peat behind
the plunge pool of the Mirefall, where the River Bracken falls into the
ground. Eight keyed rooms in two zones: the wet toll-halls the warband holds,
and the older dry work below the Deep Stair that they refuse to enter.
Uzrek Nine-Teeth runs it as a chokepoint on the
mire road — tolls, hostages, and boots on stakes. Hooks: the reeve's bounty
on the toll-stakes, the Vigil's dark wayshrine line, and the ransom of the
scholar Elowen Pell. The true danger is at the bottom:
whatever swims in the plunge pool paid for the warband's crossing, and it
expects to collect.
