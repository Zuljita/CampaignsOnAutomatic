# The Lantern Vigil

The vale chapter of a wide-scattered sacred order whose rite is simple and
absolute: keep the lanterns of the wayshrine line lit, so that no traveler
dies in the dark within sight of a light. Four professed members, a dozen
lay wick-tenders, and [[npcs/serrin-talvane|Abbess Serrin Talvane]]'s
signal-log, which records every lantern seen and missed across the vale for
eleven years.

The line is dark from the hills to the mire. The Vigil's mother-house has
not yet asked why. The abbess would very much like an answer before it does.
