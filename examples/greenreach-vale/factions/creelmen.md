# The Creelmen

GM-authored. Bracken Ford's river-trade has a second bottom, and the
Creelmen are it: a cell of perhaps eight — barge crew, two eel-wardens, one
fence — who move what the vale would rather not invoice. They are locals
first and criminals second, which is both their charm and their limit;
nothing moves on the Bracken without a Creelman knowing, and nothing the
Creelmen know is free.

Their one iron rule: the village itself is never the mark. Skarn calls this
ethics. It is actually inventory management.
