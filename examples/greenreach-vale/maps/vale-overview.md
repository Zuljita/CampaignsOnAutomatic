# Vale Overview

The GM's sketch of the vale — barley plains in the bowl, tin hills to the
northeast, the Bracken wandering down into the mire. The Warren's pin stays
unrevealed until the table finds the sinkmouth; the two settlements are common
knowledge and marked for the players from session one.
