# The Mire Toll

[[npcs/hedda-carse|Reeve Carse]]'s stake-bounty, taken at the Eel and Lantern.
The vale's answer to the tolling is coin-shaped: she wants the road open
before Peatharvest carts roll, and she is paying by the stake.

The thread runs straight at [[sites/mirefall-warren/site|Mirefall Warren]] —
but the parley taught the table that [[factions/mirefall-warband|the
warband]] is a toll operation, not a war camp, and Uzrek would rather be
bought than fought. The hidden objective is the campaign's deep hook: what is
in the plunge pool is not the warband's friend either.
