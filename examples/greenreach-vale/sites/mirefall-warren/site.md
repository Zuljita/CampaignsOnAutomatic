# Mirefall Warren

GM note: the Warren is a *toll operation*, not a war camp. Uzrek wants the
vale paying, not burning — every encounter should offer a venal way out.

<!-- oa:generated begin section="digest" -->
Mirefall Warren is a half-flooded warren of worked stone and dug peat behind
the plunge pool of the Mirefall, where the River Bracken falls into the
ground. Eight keyed rooms in two zones: the wet toll-halls the warband holds,
and the older dry work below the Deep Stair that they refuse to enter.
[[npcs/uzrek-nine-teeth|Uzrek Nine-Teeth]] runs it as a chokepoint on the
mire road — tolls, hostages, and boots on stakes. Hooks: the reeve's bounty
on the toll-stakes, the Vigil's dark wayshrine line, and the ransom of the
scholar [[npcs/elowen-pell|Elowen Pell]]. The true danger is at the bottom:
whatever swims in the plunge pool paid for the warband's crossing, and it
expects to collect.
<!-- oa:generated end -->

<!-- oa:generated begin section="getting-there" -->
Two days from [[settlements/bracken-ford|Bracken Ford]]: upriver by boat to
the edge of the marsh, then the drowned causeway on foot. The entrance is
the sinkmouth stair on the north bank of the falls at hex 0407 — swept
clean, watched from a bramble blind, and tolled. A wet alternative exists
for the bold: the eel-run under the falls surfaces inside room `room_3`,
costing a soaking and every torch the party is carrying.
