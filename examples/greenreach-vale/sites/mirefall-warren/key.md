### 1. Sinkmouth Gate

Half-drowned steps behind the falls' spray, ending at a portcullis of lashed
causeway-timbers. A bell of hammered kettle-brass hangs beside it.

**Encounter:** 2 warband gate-wards behind the timbers; they parley first —
the toll is posted, in three languages, none spelled well. Ringing the bell
brings 4 more from `room_2` in about a minute.

### 2. Toll Hall

A long worked-stone hall, shin-deep in river water, lined with the stakes and
tally-boards of the toll operation. Tally: boots, left.

**Encounter:** 6 warband skirmishers dicing on an upturned skiff. Morale
breaks if the gate falls quietly; they are paid in shares, not loyalty.

**Treasure:** the toll-chest — a locked eel-trap bolted under the skiff,
holding the month's takings in mixed copper and one signet ring the GM
should decide the owner of.

### 3. Eel-Run Landing

The wet way in. A gravel shelf where the eel-run under the falls surfaces;
everything here is soaked, including ambushes.

**Encounter:** none posted — the warband believes the run impassable. The
eels disagree, loudly, at night.

### 4. Kennel Sump

A collapsed side-gallery, ceiling open to a bat-riddled chimney. The warband
uses the flight at dusk as its clock.

**Encounter:** bat cloud (`doa_bat`) if disturbed by light or noise; harass,
not kill. The chimney is climbable by an unarmored delver with both hands
free.

### 5. Uzrek's Mud Court

The old wardens' chamber, dry above the knee, floored with plundered rugs
laid directly on the mud. A throne of stacked strongboxes, all empty, all
locked.

**Encounter:** Uzrek Nine-Teeth (see `npc_uzrek_nine_teeth`) with 2 picked
guards. He would rather tax the party than fight it, and rather fight it
than let it downstairs.

**Treasure:** worn under his sash — the Longreach adit charter-seal, which
is worth more to the right buyer in Greenreach than everything in room 7.

### 6. The Wet Cell

A grated alcove at water level, miserable on purpose.

**Encounter:** the captive scholar Elowen Pell (`npc_elowen_pell`), held for
ransom nobody has figured out how to post. She has counted every guard shift
and mapped the warren in scratch-marks under the grate's lip.

### 7. Plunder Nook

Dry-stacked stone closet behind a rug-hung doorway; the warband's actual
hoard, smelling of tar and river mud.

**Treasure:** bales of tolled trade goods marked for Creelmen pickup — the
tar-marks match barge-brands a sharp-eyed delver has seen in Bracken Ford.

### 8. The Deep Stair

A descending stair of pre-warband stonework, scoured clean by periodic
flooding. The warband bricked the landing with causeway gravestones, then
painted the bricks with warding daubs, then stopped coming down here at all.

**Encounter:** nothing on the stair. Below the waterline the stair opens
into the plunge-pool deeps — the domain of `enraged_eggplant_aboleth`, which
is out of the site's danger band and should end an expedition, not a
campaign. The daubs are on the *inside* face of the bricks.
