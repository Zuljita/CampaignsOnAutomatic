# Elowen Pell

Greenreach charter-scholar, hired by the abbess to survey the ruined priory
records, taken on the mire road in spring by guards who were paid to lose
her. Four months in the wet cell have produced the best map of Mirefall
Warren in existence, scratched under the grate's lip, and a working theory
about the Deep Stair she will trade only for her freedom — and only to
someone who lets her *finish the survey*.
