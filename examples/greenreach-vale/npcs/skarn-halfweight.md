# Skarn Halfweight

GM-authored. Fence, ferry-share owner, and the Eel and Lantern's most
permanent fixture. Skarn buys what the river brings and asks the river no
questions. His public ledger balances; his private one is the vale's true
map — every bale the warband tolls, every barge-brand, every name.

He is genuinely fond of Bracken Ford, in the way a tick is fond of a dog,
and would be sincerely wounded to watch it burn from the far bank.
