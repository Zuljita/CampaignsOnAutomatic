# Reeve Hedda Carse

GM-authored. Bracken Ford's reeve: gray plait, ferry-callused hands, and a
memory for tallies that frightens honest people slightly and dishonest
people a great deal. She posted the bounty on the mire-road toll-stakes out
of the village strongbox without asking the moot, and will account for it,
to the penny, when someone dares to raise it.

She attends the Vigil's chapel every dawn and believes exactly as much of it
as gets the lanterns lit. What she wants from delvers is simple: the road
open, the boots off the stakes, and no heroics expensive enough to need a
song.
